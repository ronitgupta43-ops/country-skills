# Tax for creators in India (Tax Year 2026-27)

## All of this is taxable business/professional income
Brand fees, YouTube AdSense, affiliate commissions, memberships/Super Chats, paid courses, merch, event fees, and gifted products, trips and hotel stays at fair market value. Products genuinely returned after the review are treated differently; keep proof of return. Tax is at slab rates; use india-income-tax for the slab calculation.

## TDS brands deduct (claim it as credit when filing)
- Cash fees for content: usually 10% as fees for professional services (old section 194J). Some agencies deduct 1 to 2% as a contract payment (old 194C).
- Gifted products and perks: 10% on the value once a brand's benefits to you cross Rs 20,000 in a year (old 194R). The brand may ask you to pay this TDS amount before sending the product.
- From 1 April 2026 these are in section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 with the same rates; old section numbers still appear on many forms.
- Without PAN on file, TDS can be 20%. Check Form 26AS / AIS every quarter and match it to your income.

## GST
- Registration is mandatory once aggregate turnover crosses Rs 20 lakh (Rs 10 lakh in special category states). Count everything: brand fees, AdSense, affiliate income.
- Creator services are 18% GST. Charge it on invoices to Indian brands; same state CGST+SGST, other state IGST (use india-gst to calculate).
- YouTube AdSense and payments from foreign brands are usually export of services: zero-rated. File a LUT to invoice without IGST, and keep FIRC/bank proof of foreign currency receipt.
- Barter deals can also attract GST on the value exchanged.

## Filing
- ITR-3 (with books of account) is the safe default. Use profession code 16021 (social media influencer).
- Presumptive schemes (44AD / 44ADA) can lower the paperwork, but whether influencing qualifies for 44ADA is disputed; tell the user to confirm with a CA before choosing.
- Advance tax is due in instalments (15 Jun, 15 Sep, 15 Dec, 15 Mar) if the year's tax after TDS is Rs 10,000 or more.

## Expenses usually deductible (keep bills)
Camera, phone, lights, mic (as depreciation), editing software, internet and phone share, studio rent share, travel for shoots, editor/manager fees, ads you run for your own channel.

## Records
A sheet with: date, brand, deliverable, invoice no., amount, GST, TDS deducted, product value (for gifts), payment date. Save contracts and product invoices.

End with: rules depend on how each deal is structured; confirm with a CA.
