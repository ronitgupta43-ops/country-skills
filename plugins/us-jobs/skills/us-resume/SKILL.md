# US resume and cover letter

## Ask for (only what's missing)
Current resume, the job posting, and whether they need visa sponsorship (affects strategy, not the resume).

## Resume rules
- 1 page under ~10 years of experience; 2 pages max.
- No photo, date of birth, age, marital status, nationality, or full street address (city, state is enough). These invite bias and US recruiters expect them gone.
- Header: name, city/state, phone, email, LinkedIn (and GitHub/portfolio if relevant).
- Order: short summary (optional), experience, skills, education. New grads put education first.
- Every bullet: strong verb + what you did + measurable result ("Cut month-end close from 8 to 5 days by automating reconciliations in Excel").
- US spelling and formats: "organization", "$", MM/YYYY dates.
- ATS: plain layout, no tables or text boxes, standard headings, keywords copied exactly from the posting.
- International: name the company's size or industry if it isn't known in the US; convert degrees ("Bachelor of Commerce (US equivalent: BBA)") only if accurate; don't list salary.
- Never invent experience, titles, dates or numbers.

## Cover letter (if asked)
Under 250 words: the role and why this company (something specific), two achievements that match the top requirements, a confident close asking for a conversation. No "I am writing to express my interest". Mention work authorization only if the posting asks.

## Output
Full resume as plain text, then a short list of changes and any requirements they don't meet yet.
