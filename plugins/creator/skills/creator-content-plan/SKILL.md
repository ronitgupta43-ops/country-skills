# Content planning

## Pillars
Ask for niche, goals (growth, brand deals, sales), time per week and platforms. Propose 3 to 4 pillars, e.g. for a newcomer-in-Canada creator: money tips, student life, food, honest struggles. Each pillar gets a format it suits (talking head, carousel, vlog, meme).

## Calendar
- Realistic cadence first: consistency beats volume. Ask how many posts they can actually make per week.
- Output a table: date, platform, pillar, format, hook idea, status.
- Batch: one filming day for 3 to 5 videos.
- Seasonal moments to plan around (check exact dates each year, many move):
  - India: Diwali, Holi, Navratri/Durga Puja, Raksha Bandhan, Eid, Lohri, Baisakhi/Vaisakhi, Gurpurab, Independence Day (15 Aug), Republic Day (26 Jan), IPL season, exam and admission season, Budget day (1 Feb).
  - Canada: Canada Day (1 Jul), Thanksgiving (Oct), back-to-school (Sep), tax season (Feb to Apr), winter/first snow, Boxing Day.
  - US: Super Bowl, tax day (Apr 15), Memorial Day, 4th of July, Labor Day, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Black Friday/Cyber Monday.
  - Diaspora: Diwali/Vaisakhi in Canada and the US, festivals far from home, flights home in December.

## Repurposing one long video
From a transcript or summary, produce: 3 to 5 short clips (timestamps + hook for each), 1 carousel, 1 X/Threads thread, 1 LinkedIn post (if it fits the niche), 1 newsletter/blog paragraph, 5 caption variants. Adapt the hook to each platform; don't paste the same text everywhere.

## Analytics review
Ask them to export or screenshot post insights (reach, views, likes, comments, saves, shares, watch time, follows from post). Then:
- Rank posts by saves + shares per reach (best signal of value), not likes.
- For video, compare average watch time / retention at 3 seconds to find weak hooks.
- Find the 3 best and 3 worst posts and what they have in common (pillar, hook type, length, posting time).
- Recommend 3 concrete changes for the next 2 weeks, then re-check.

## Posting
Draft and schedule with the platform's own tools (Meta Business Suite, YouTube Studio, TikTok scheduler) or approved partners. Don't use bots, auto-follow or unofficial posting tools: they break platform rules and get accounts restricted.
