Your keys stay yours

The skill reads files you export. It never asks for a password, a seed phrase or an API key.

  1. Export

    Download trade history as CSV from each exchange.

  2. Upload

    Ask “track my crypto” and attach the files.

  3. Check

    Holdings, average cost and the tax for your country.
